# Rulers (reference pack)

Dates only so far: `dates.json` holds who reigned when, by country, for the app's "who was on the throne" facts. Add sections here (format: docs/KNOWLEDGE_FORMAT.md) if a site needs more than the dates.

Coverage (October 2026): England/Britain 1066–2026, Scotland 1058–1707, France 1060–2026 (kings, then republics), Holy Roman Empire 1438–1806, Spain 1479–2026, Austria 1804–1918. Lists for current rulers and republics end at 2026, because the format has no "to date": extend the end year each year.

Sources: standard reign dates as given by the Royal Family's official site (royal.uk, "Kings and Queens of England and Britain"), the Oxford Dictionary of National Biography and Encyclopaedia Britannica. Disputed reigns are labelled in the list: Stephen (disputed with Matilda), Lady Jane Grey (1553), the 1470–1471 restoration of Henry VI, and Scottish interregnums (1290–1292, 1296–1306).
