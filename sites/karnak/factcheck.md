# Karnak: fact-check report

Checked 23 November 2026 by an independent fact-checker (fresh context, report only; no content file was edited).

Scope: `tours/karnak.json` (all 127 bullets with `imp` 4 or 5 or `verify`, every timeline event and its `more`, stop date labels), `knowledge/karnak/notes.md` (read in full), `knowledge/karnak/dates.json` (every thing, person and `owners` row), the rulers pack (Egypt, Rome, Egypt after the Roman period), and the `egyptian-temples`, `egyptian-gods` and `ancient-building` packs.

Shaw's chronology was checked against the Bibliotheca Alexandrina Antiquities Museum's "Ancient Egyptian Kings List", which states it follows Shaw (ed.), The Oxford History of Ancient Egypt (2000): https://antiquities.bibalex.org/Attachments/images/Ancient%20Egyptian%20Kings%20List%20-%20English(2).pdf (below: "BA/Shaw list"). That list stops at 332 BC; Ptolemaic rows were checked against a secondary page that says it follows Shaw.

Confidentiality: nothing in the files or sources is internal-only or confidential to Roboverse Reply. All material is public.

Most serious problems:
1. **Red Chapel date and an impossible date.** The site says Hatshepsut's year 17 is "about 1457 BC". By the site's own rule (her years count from 1479 BC, the rule used to put her year 16 obelisks at 1464 BC), year 17 is about 1463 BC. `dates.json` also has Hatshepsut "having the Red Chapel begun or finished" in 1457 BC, a year after her death in 1458 BC.
2. **Amenhotep II and Thutmose IV are not Shaw.** Shaw gives Amenhotep II 1427–1400 BC and Thutmose IV 1400–1390 BC. The rulers pack has Amenhotep II 1425–1400; the site notes and dates have 1427–1401; `dates.json` gives Thutmose IV 1401–1391. STATUS.md's statement that Shaw has 1425–1400 is itself wrong.
3. **Several rulers-pack rows depart from Shaw**: the Sixteenth and Seventeenth Dynasties, Seqenenre, the whole Twenty-first Dynasty, Shoshenq V, Psamtik I, Alexander IV, Ptolemy I and II, and Ptolemy VIII.
4. **Opet festival** in the temples pack ends in 1070 BC, but it continued at least into the Roman period (and the site itself links it to Nectanebo I's avenue of 380–362 BC).
5. **Two hedges that are no longer needed:** the Hittite treaty replica at the UN is confirmed by the UN's own gift catalogue (Turkey, 24 September 1970), and the ministry merger is confirmed as 22 December 2019.
6. **Papyrus Harris I percentages** (80 per cent of land, 86 per cent of livestock) are on Wikipedia, but sourced there to Breasted (1906), not to Grandet as the notes say.
