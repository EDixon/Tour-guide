# Karnak: source list

Precinct of Amun-Re, Karnak, Luxor, Egypt, plus the Open Air Museum.
Compiled 2026-11-22. All sources are public. No internal-only or confidential Roboverse Reply material is involved.

Status key: **(fetched)** means the page was opened and read in this session. *(search)* means it appeared in search results but was not opened (several official sites returned HTTP 403 or 503 to the fetch tool, so they are unverified beyond the search snippet). Nothing here has been checked against a printed page, and no page numbers are given.

Important correction to the brief: UCLA's Digital Karnak is now hosted at **digitalkarnak.ucsc.edu** (UC Santa Cruz). The old dlib.etc.ucla.edu address was not returned by search and its fetch failed (503). Treat the UCSC address as the live one.

## Disagreements and uncertainties

- **Hypostyle columns, count:** 134 in all sources found (Wikipedia, Memphis). Not in conflict, but 134 is "122 plus 12" and the 1899 collapse is reported as eleven columns by most sources, with one 1904 report listing twelve.
- **Hypostyle column heights:** Memphis: central 20 m, others 14 m. Wikipedia "Karnak": central 21 m, others 10 m. Wikipedia "Great Hypostyle Hall": central 24 m, others 14 m, 10 m circumference. Three different sets. The Memphis project page is the best-supported (Peter Brand's project); the Karnak article's "10 m" for the smaller columns looks like a confusion with circumference. Flag in app text.
- **Hatshepsut obelisk height:** Wikipedia "nearly 30 m"; a list gives 29.56 m; other pages 29.5 to 30 m. Brief's 29.5 m is plausible. Unverified against a primary or scholarly measurement.
- **Hatshepsut obelisk weight:** 323 tons (Explore Luxor), 343 tonnes (The National), 374 tons (an 1895 to 1896 report). Ton versus tonne differences are not explained by sources. Say "about 320 to 375 tonnes, estimates vary".
- **Unfinished Obelisk, Aswan:** about 41.75 m and nearly 1,090 tonnes (Wikipedia list) versus about 42 m and 1,200 tons (Explore Luxor, probably short tons).
- **Opening hours:** official page snippet says 06:00 to 17:00. The brief mentions 17:30. No source for 17:30 was found. Third-party sources say last entry about 16:00. Hours change seasonally (winter or summer) and are not confirmed.
- **Ticket price:** 600 EGP adult, 300 EGP student per 2026 guides; an older figure of 200 EGP. Official price not confirmed. Mut temple is a separate ticket (about 200 EGP foreign adult, third-party).
- **Pylon dates:** not checked in this pass. Memphis gives Horemheb and Amenhotep III for the pylons either side of the Hypostyle Hall, with Amenhotep III given as "ca. 1346 to 1349 BCE", which is not a usable reign date. Needs a scholarly chronology (Digital Karnak or Sullivan 2010).
- **Cachette:** more than 750 or nearly 800 stone statues; 17,000 or 18,000 bronzes. Dates 1903 to 1904 for the main find; one source says excavation continued to 1907. Legrain's own diaries are lost.
- **Hypostyle date:** Wikipedia says built under Seti I with the southern wing decorated by Ramesses II, c. 1290 to 1224 BCE. Memphis says construction may have begun under Ramesses I.
- **Sound and Light:** duration (one hour versus two hours) and start times unconfirmed beyond a 2017 user note.
- **Names:** Senusret, Senwosret, Sesostris; Sety, Seti; Amen-Re, Amun-Ra. House style should be fixed.
