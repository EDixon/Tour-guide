Survey of 12 October 2026. This is an existing site, so the researchers **extend and correct**: keep bullets that are right, fix ones that aren't, and fill gaps. Sources are listed in `sites/wrest-park/sources.md`. Dated things and people already exist in `knowledge/wrest-park/dates.json`; use those ids and add `who`, `events` and links rather than duplicating them.

General rules for every batch:
- **Upgrade sources.** Replace "general knowledge" and Wikipedia with site-specific sources wherever a bullet or section is about Wrest (CLAUDE.md rule). Where no better source exists, keep the claim, mark it `verify`, and say so.
- **Write dates in full** ("1758–1760", "about 1735").
- **Toddler tasks:** two or three `kids` per stop that a two-year-old can genuinely find, each with a photo from `sites/wrest-park/photos.json` where one shows it.
- **Timeline `more`:** 1–3 sentences, under 60 words, for each event listed against the batch. Use the year and label exactly as given.
- Several searches during the survey came back as summaries only (marked *(search)* in `sources.md`). Open the pages and confirm.
