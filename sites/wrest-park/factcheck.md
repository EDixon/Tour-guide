# Wrest Park: fact-check

Independent check, 12 October 2026. Report only: no content file was edited.

**Summary.** About 560 claims checked: **wrong 10**, **sources disagree 27**, **unsupported 12**, **confirmed about 510**.

What was checked: all 66 bullets with `imp` 4–5, all 42 `verify` bullets and all 23 `gk` bullets (107 bullets, after overlaps), plus about 30 other bullets, all 37 timeline events and their `more` text, all 32 `owners` rows, every `things` and `people` row in `knowledge/wrest-park/dates.json`, about 25 rulers rows, about 60 rows across the habsburgs, garden-styles and farm-machinery dates files, and roughly a fifth of the notes sentences (site and packs). Several look and note lines were also checked against stop positions.

**Sources opened in full** (not just search summaries): 27 Historic England list entries via British Listed Buildings; English Heritage history, war, nurses, nurse-news, revitalising, collection, treasures, statues press release, top-dogs, visit, facilities and access pages; the 2024 orientation leaflet (PDF); VCH Bedford vol. 2 pp. 325–333; 13 Bedfordshire Archives pages and one catalogue entry; Parks & Gardens UK (Wrest Park and Thomas Wright); Heritage Fund news page; Art Fund (Diana); Google Arts & Culture ("Women's Designs", "Lost and Found"); Historic England Research Reports 6/2013 and 74/2018; Albion Archaeology 2007/47 (Old Park Weir); AML Report 28/97; History of Parliament (Anthony Grey); Hansard, 1 November 1960; Silsoe Research Institute history; MERL SR SRI catalogue (PDF); four Farmers Weekly articles; Tommy's Footprints; British Sundial Society S2125, S5169 and S6338; Wikishire, Silsoe; Geograph 871840; FPCR; Linda Hall Library; Warwick; Oxford "Wrest Circle"; the koreascience abstract (Silsoe cube); and Wikipedia (Wrest Park and about 30 biography and topic pages).

**Could not open:** History Today (403), The Robot Report (403), the IAgrE citation, Art UK, the BCPC 1999 PDF and the EH phased-plans PDF (not tried, because of its size). Historic England's own site was not used. The register entry 1000113 is empty on British Listed Buildings, so I used the Parks & Gardens UK copy of the register text.
